Europa

-icy moon with red streaks of mineral/salt water. the rig is located in one of these chasms/streaks
-mysterious element called tenerium
-occasional acid rain
-Jupiter always visible in the sky, and always in the same place due to being tidally locked
-extremely cold and lacks oxygen; people going outside are required to put on special suits

Tenerium

-red, glowing crystals found in the caverns deep underneath the red streaks of Europa
-believed to be a source of infinite energy, but not yet fully understood
-extremely unstable and dangerous when exposed to oxygen
-its extraction and usage is strictly banned by UCOEH (United Committee of Off-Earth Humans) 
-extracted and used by SOMA to create experimental planet-scale WMDs in secret research operation located in Jupiter's eye

SOMA

-company running the rig, as well as many other extraterrestrial industries around the outer half of the solar system
-considered to be the single most powerful human organization in the solar system (including countries and corporations)
-starting as a multi government funded space exploration agency, its influence and wealth has surpassed any other entity on Earth or anywhere else in the solar system - owing its riches to the near infinite sources of diamonds on Jupiter and Uranus.
-millions live their entire lives on SOMA-funded stations around the asteroid belt, knowing very little of Earth
-massive constant stream of people born and raised in the asteroid belt colonies are hired for mining and research operations around Jupiter and Uranus
-has future ambitions of becoming the sole representative entity for humanity, with the species as a whole united under one collective enterprise
-many secret military operations and r&d projects have been deployed to make this an eventual reality

The Rig

-one of the first mining facilities installed on Europa, shortly after the discovery of tenerium deep near its core
-officially stated mission is to mine and extract salts and minerals from the red waters of the icy moon
-it's true purpose is to extract tenerium and prep the material for tests on Jupiter
-employs a loyal crew of long-time scientists and engineers, as well as many other seasonal/temporary workers
-fully self-sustainable, requiring no imports for food or oxygen
-due to difficulty of landing from strong winds, shipments are quite infrequent but contain immense amount of cargo. thus, these shipments are especially well-guarded and closely monitored to deter jovian pirates
